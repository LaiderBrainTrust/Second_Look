## Demo path (target under 3 minutes)

Before presenting, run `npm run doctor`, `npm test`, and `npm run build`. Start `npm run dev` and refresh external evidence once. This is a local synthetic demo, not a live securities transaction. Use an existing saved review if demonstrating a previously fetched result; say so. Never relabel a saved result as a fresh live call.

1. **0:00–0:25 — The question.** “You own an interest in a gaming fund. Someone offers you $700,000 for it. The last report is weeks old. What exactly are you selling?” Say the fund and exposures are fictional; Steam observations and Akta company research are real.
2. **0:25–0:55 — Ryan's domain.** Inspect a game he knows. Open the sample, explain what the players are saying and what the sample cannot tell us. Open the Akta studio profile: who develops/publishes the game is not the same as who owns the economic rights. No deterioration is also a legitimate finding.
3. **0:55–1:25 — Turn evidence into a question.** Select the manager-question button. The app saves the exact evidence in Roundtable, assigns its reviewer, calls Nicrron, and saves the result against this offer revision. PRISM traces that real model call only when configured and confirmed. Review the proposed question; explicitly share it.
4. **1:25–1:50 — Close the loop.** Switch to Fund manager. Enter the labeled fictional response confirming a $100,000 distribution and its entitlement. Return to Investor and approve the current proposed response. This is simulated role switching, not proof of real participant authentication.
5. **1:50–2:20 — The consequence.** Switch to Buyer and change who gets the distribution while retaining the $700,000 purchase payment. Return to Investor: the old approval is historical, and the visible cash difference is $100,000. On a fresh database the initial direction is seller → buyer, so gross receipts fall from $800,000 to $700,000. If the saved case already starts with buyer entitlement, change to seller first; do not claim the inverse direction is a loss.
6. **2:20–2:45 — What survives.** Refresh the page, open Review the deal, and show saved history and the printable decision record. “A chat can help understand a game. This keeps the evidence, the unanswered question, the participants' responses, and the exact deal version connected.” Do not claim a moat solely from this prototype.

The workflow is the product; the game ranking is an investigation aid. `fundNavExposurePercent` always means a fictional share of fund NAV, never an equity stake in a game developer. It cannot be multiplied into revenue to estimate LP proceeds.
