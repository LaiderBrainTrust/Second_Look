Decision: keep the six-hour product narrow. Do not pitch generic AI research, memory, MCP or multiplayer as unique. The comparison below uses vendors' own pages; it is not a hands-on evaluation or proof that they lack an unadvertised feature.

## Pitch we can defend

“Before you sell your interest in a gaming fund, follow what is happening in the games through to what cash actually belongs to you. Ask the manager what the public data cannot establish. When the offer changes who gets that cash, see the effect and which approval needs another look.”

Real Steam/Akta observations start the inquiry; they do not establish ownership, distributable revenue or valuation. The manager answer and distribution in this prototype are fictional and labeled. The saved decision records and invalidation logic actually execute.

## The hard objection

“Couldn't DiligenceVault, Carta or an internal agent workflow add this?” Yes. Today's work is a concrete workflow hypothesis, not a moat. The unproven advantage is a short, understandable review that connects domain evidence, manager responses and changing sale terms without losing their lineage. We must test that with actual LP reviewers, and establish which existing product already handles their case.

## What earns differentiation after today

Ask a reviewer to walk through the last changed-term LP sale using their existing tools. Identify the missing or repeated step before proposing ours. Compare time to answer, source/term omissions, and whether every affected reviewer gets the correct revised packet. Seek a redacted pilot and willingness to pay; do not count a positive demo reaction as either.

## Consequences for this build

- Keep the game → studio → manager answer → cash entitlement → sale term visible.
- Preserve the exact supporting question and answer with each approval.
- Explain that current roles are simulated; authentic cross-organization use remains unbuilt.
- Do not add broad company discovery, a second leaderboard, or an MCP feature solely to claim novelty.
- Do not claim alpha, unique market coverage, automatic fair value, or guaranteed savings.
