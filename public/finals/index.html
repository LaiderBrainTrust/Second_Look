0:00–0:20 · Would you take the higher offer?

Imagine you’re selling your share of a fund. One buyer offers $700,000. Another offers $760,000. You’d probably take the higher offer. But what if that means giving up money the fund is about to pay you?

0:20–0:45 · First, who keeps the fund payout?

We built Second Look to bring those missing details into the decision. These are screenshots from our working app, using a fictional fund. We start with outside research, here about a video game company, and turn it into a question for the fund. We keep the answer with the offer instead of losing it in an email thread.

0:45–1:30 · The lower offer leaves you $40,000 more.

In this example, the fund confirms a $100,000 payout. The first offer lets you keep it, so you receive $800,000. The second gives it to the buyer, leaving you $760,000. That’s $40,000 less, before tax. Change the answer or terms, and the earlier checks need reviewing. Akta supplies company research, Nicrron drafts the question, Roundtable keeps the research history, and PRISM traces the AI call. We want to build this into the place investors and their advisers review every fund sale, from the first question to the final terms.
