# Second Look product experience

Mat authorized replacement of the confusing engineering screen on October 24.
Mode: Operate. One case, one clearly named next action, supporting detail on demand.

Use a warm paper workspace with dark ink, a restrained green action color and a serif heading to give the financial decision room to breathe. Use flat sections and dividing rules rather than nested dashboard cards. The one cash diagram explains the current sale; no decorative charts or invented metrics.

Tokens: paper #f6f5f0, surface #ffffff, ink #172d27, muted #56645e, rule #dce2da, action #215c42, soft action #e9f1e9, warning #74420f, warning surface #fff2de. Body DM Sans with sans fallback; headings Libre Caslon Display with Georgia fallback. 16px body, 1.55 line height. Controls at least 44px tall. Explicit focus rings, labeled forms, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, responsive single column below 760px.

Primary navigation: Deal, Evidence, Conversation, Decision. Invitation and case routes stay in the URL. Technical provider details live under Service details. Fictional example disclosure remains visible but compact. Never pretend comparison quotes are the deal being approved.
