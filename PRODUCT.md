# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Web. Existing React/TypeScript/Vite frontend and Node/Express/SQLite backend.

## Users and purpose
An investor considering a sale of a private-fund interest needs to understand cash terms, collect a fund-manager answer and preserve the exact decision. Manager and buyer participants have separate case permissions. These facts follow Mat's approved plan and current API contract.

## Current scope
Compare entered sale terms, inspect external evidence, ask a manager, record a review, and export the record. Optional X1 access imports selected private document excerpts. No valuation claim, sale execution or money movement. The team example is fictional, visibly identified and distinct from individual accounts.

## Success for this release
Mat explicitly requested taking over the unshipped UI: a nontechnical person should understand what the app does, what matters in the current case and what action to take, without explanation from an engineer. Account and invitation flows must use real APIs. All existing authorization and revision checks stay intact.

## Constraints
One hour to the presentation. Preserve teammate work. Do not add capabilities or unsupported commercial claims. No sensitive information in repository assets. Use existing dependencies and sponsor adapters.
